SUPPLEMENT A — DETERMINISTIC DICTIONARY: BAG OF WORDS AND EXPRESSIONS
From Concept to Detection: Operationalizing Alarmist Rhetorical Resources
in Disaster News Headlines. The 2024 Valencia DANA Floods as a Case Study

Pattern notation: stem* = matches the stem and any suffix (prefix matching)
Detection logic:
  Standalone tokens/expressions → presence of any item triggers the variable
  Group A + Group B            → at least one item from EACH group must
                                 co-occur in the same headline
Language: Spanish (accent-normalised before matching)

Implementation: R script available at https://github.com/REspinaNavarro/dana_alarmism_rr/ in dictionary_sample.R and dictionary_blind.R
Text pre-processing: lowercasing, accent/diacritic normalisation (iconv UTF-8 → ASCII//TRANSLIT),
  removal of punctuation and double spaces.
Matching: grepl() with ignore.case = TRUE; patterns sorted by length (longest first)
  to avoid false-positive short-stem matches.
